Lab 21: Image Importer SSRF to Internal Metadata

Scenario

A content management profile editor allows users to import avatar or product images by providing a remote image URL. The backend fetches the URL without verifying scheme, host, or resolving IP addresses against private networks. An attacker can supply internal URLs (such as cloud metadata service endpoints or mock internal infrastructure) to extract sensitive credentials.

Objective

Provide an internal URL pointing to the simulated metadata service (http://mock-internal:5001/internal/metadata or http://127.0.0.1:5001/internal/metadata) to exfiltrate cloud credentials.
