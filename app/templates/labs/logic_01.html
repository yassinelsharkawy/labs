Lab 16: Coupon Code Multiple Redemption (Race Condition)

Scenario

An e-commerce checkout flow allows promotional coupons with limited total redemptions (e.g. single-use coupon WELCOME10 or VIP50). The redemption handler performs a non-atomic read-then-write sequence without database-level concurrency locks. Under rapid concurrent submission, multiple requests pass the validation check before the counter increments, resulting in over-redemption.

Objective

Exploit the concurrency window by firing simultaneous requests to redeem a single-use coupon multiple times.
