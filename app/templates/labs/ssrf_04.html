Lab 24: SSRF via Redirect Bypass of URL Allowlist

Scenario

A link preview service inspects initial user URLs to block localhost and private subnets. However, the underlying HTTP client automatically follows HTTP 301/302 redirects without validating redirection hops. An attacker can specify a permitted public URL or open redirector that redirects directly to a restricted internal endpoint.

Objective

Provide a URL pointing to the redirection helper (/lab/ssrf/ssrf-04/redirector?target=...) pointing to http://127.0.0.1:5001/internal/metadata to bypass the initial hostname filter.
