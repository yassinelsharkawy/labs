Lab 25: SSRF via URL Parsing Inconsistency

Scenario

An asset fetcher filters destination hostnames by parsing userinfo credentials. Due to a custom parsing error, the filter splits on @ and extracts the prefix segment rather than the actual target destination host, allowing an attacker to supply URLs such as http://allowed.example.com@127.0.0.1:5001/internal/metadata.

Objective

Craft a URL with @ notation that passes the validation parser and reaches internal services.
