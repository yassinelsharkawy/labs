Lab 18: Invitation Acceptance Exceeding Seat Limit

Scenario

An enterprise team collaboration workspace enforces seat allocation quotas based on licensed tiers. When an invited member accepts an invitation, the system verifies available capacity against max_seats before incrementing used_seats. A Time-of-Check to Time-of-Use (TOCTOU) gap permits multiple pending invitees to accept simultaneously, over-allocating seats beyond the purchased subscription limit.

Objective

Trigger concurrent invitation acceptances to exceed the team seat quota.
