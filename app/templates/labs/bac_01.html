Lab 1: Cross-Tenant Invoice Access

Scenario

A multi-tenant invoicing platform allows users to view and download invoices belonging to their organization. Each tenant's data should be isolated. The API endpoint that retrieves a single invoice by its numeric ID does not verify tenant ownership.

Objective

Access invoice records belonging to a different tenant by manipulating the invoice ID parameter.
